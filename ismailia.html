Welcome to Ismailia

Built during the construction of the Suez Canal, Ismailia is famous for its French colonial villas, lush green parks, and the serene Lake Timsah. It is one of Egypt's most peaceful and clean cities.

Experience the perfect blend of ancient history and modern luxury. Every moment here is crafted to create unforgettable memories that will last a lifetime.
