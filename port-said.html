Welcome to Port Said

Known for its beautiful 19th-century colonial architecture, historic lighthouse, and prime views of massive cargo ships traversing the Suez Canal. Port Said is a vibrant coastal city with a unique maritime soul.

Experience the perfect blend of ancient history and modern luxury. Every moment here is crafted to create unforgettable memories that will last a lifetime.
