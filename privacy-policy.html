Privacy Notice for Travel Planning Inquiries

At Horus Guide Travel, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Notice explains how we handle your personal data when you use our contact and travel planning forms.

1. What Data We Collect

We collect the information you voluntarily provide in our inquiry forms, which may include your name, age group, email address, nationality, phone/WhatsApp number, travel destinations, budget range, preferred travel dates, trip duration, accommodation style, and any optional special requests you submit.

2. How We Use and Process Your Data

Your data is processed solely to communicate with you and design your custom travel itinerary in Egypt.

  • No Database Archiving: We do not store or archive your personal details in a permanent website database. Your sensitive data is not kept in a public or private cloud database connected directly to the web code.
  • How It Works: We use Web3Forms as our form processing provider. When you click submit, your data is securely transmitted directly to our official email address (turkayduru7@gmail.com) and is not retained permanently on the form provider's servers.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketers or advertisers. Your seyahat details are only visible to our internal travel organizers and local guides who work directly on setting up your trip.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & Global Regulations)

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of any emails or communication history containing your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us directly via email at turkayduru7@gmail.com.
