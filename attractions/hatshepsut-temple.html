About

Discover Temple of Hatshepsut

The Mortuary Temple of Hatshepsut is a masterpiece of ancient classical architecture. Built against the towering cliffs of Deir el-Bahari, this terraced temple honors one of Egypt's rare female pharaohs who ruled as a king, depicting her trade expeditions and divine birth on its walls.

Designed by the royal architect Senenmut, the temple consists of three layered terraces reaching 30 meters high, connected by long ramps and surrounded by gardens. Hatshepsut ruled for over 20 years during Egypt's golden age. After her death, her successor attempted to erase her name and images from the temple, but the architectural brilliance of the site preserved her legacy.

Historical Timeline

c. 1479 BC
Hatshepsut commissions the construction of her mortuary temple at Deir el-Bahari.
c. 1460 BC
The temple is completed, featuring beautiful painted reliefs of the Punt expedition.
c. 1458 BC
Thutmose III ascends the throne; later, efforts to scratch out Hatshepsut's images begin.
19th century
Archaeologists begin excavating the temple from under deep piles of rock debris.
1961
The Polish-Egyptian Archaeological Mission begins long-term restoration of the terraces.
1979
The temple is inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site along with ancient Thebes.

Essential Info

Visitor Tips

Everything you need to know for a perfect visit.

🌅

Arrive Early

The temple gets extremely hot by midday and there is no shade on the ramps. 6-8 AM is best.

🧴

Sun Protection

Bring high-SPF sunscreen, a wide-brimmed hat, and sunglasses. The white limestone reflects intense light.

💧

Bring Water

Keep a bottle of cold water with you; there are no shops inside the archaeological area.

🚶

Electric Taf-Taf

A shuttle train is available to carry visitors from the parking lot to the temple gate.

🎫

Ticket Purchase

Buy tickets at the main visitors' center before boarding the shuttle train.

📷

Photography Tip

The best panoramic shot is from the parking lot approach, capturing the entire facade against the cliffs.

Best Time to Visit

November to February. Always go early morning to avoid extreme heat.
