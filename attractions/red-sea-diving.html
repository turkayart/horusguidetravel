About

Discover Red Sea Diving

The Red Sea is consistently ranked among the world's top diving destinations. Crystal-clear waters with visibility exceeding 30 meters, vibrant coral reefs hosting over 1,000 species of fish, and iconic dive sites like Ras Mohammed, the Blue Hole, and the SS Thistlegorm wreck make this an underwater paradise for both beginners and experienced divers.

The Red Sea's coral reefs are among the oldest and most diverse marine ecosystems on Earth, with some reef formations dating back millions of years. Pioneering underwater explorer Jacques Cousteau first brought global attention to the Red Sea's wonders in the 1950s and 60s. Since then, Egypt's Red Sea coast has developed into one of the world's premier diving destinations, with hundreds of dive centers operating from Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, Dahab, and Marsa Alam.

Historical Timeline

Millions of years
The Red Sea's coral reef ecosystems begin forming as the sea gradually widens.
1941
The SS Thistlegorm, a WWII cargo ship, sinks — later becoming the world's most famous wreck dive.
1956
Jacques Cousteau films 'The Silent World' featuring Red Sea footage, sparking global interest.
1983
Ras Mohammed is declared Egypt's first national marine park.
2000s
Egypt becomes one of the top 5 scuba diving destinations in the world.
Present
Conservation efforts protect marine biodiversity across designated reef reserves.

Essential Info

Visitor Tips

Everything you need to know for a perfect visit.

🤿

Best Visibility

September to November offers the clearest water with 30m+ visibility.

🐠

Don't Touch

Never touch, stand on, or take anything from coral reefs. It's fragile and protected by law.

📋

PADI Centers

Only dive with PADI or SSI certified centers. Dahab has the cheapest courses.

☀️

Sunscreen

Use reef-safe sunscreen only. Chemical sunscreens damage coral ecosystems.

🌊

Currents

Some dive sites have strong currents. Always follow your divemaster's briefing.

📸

Underwater Camera

Rent an underwater camera from your dive center if you don't have one — worth it!

Best Time to Visit

Diving is year-round! Sep-Nov has best visibility. Summer has warmer water and more marine life.
