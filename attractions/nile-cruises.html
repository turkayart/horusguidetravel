About

Discover Nile Cruises

A Nile cruise between Luxor and Aswan is the quintessential Egypt experience. Glide along the same waters that carried the pharaohs' barges, stopping at ancient temples and colorful villages along the way. From the comfort of your floating hotel, watch the timeless landscape of golden sand, green palm groves, and ancient monuments unfold.

Travel along the Nile has been central to Egyptian civilization for over 5,000 years. The pharaohs used elaborately decorated barges to travel between temples. In the 19th century, Thomas Cook pioneered luxury Nile steamship travel, popularizing Egypt as a tourist destination for European aristocrats. Today, modern cruise ships offer 5-star amenities while following the same ancient route.

Historical Timeline

c. 3000 BC
Ancient Egyptians begin using the Nile as their primary highway for trade and travel.
c. 1479 BC
Queen Hatshepsut sends trading expeditions down the Nile to the land of Punt.
1869
Thomas Cook organizes the first modern Nile cruise for paying tourists.
1920s
Agatha Christie writes 'Death on the Nile' inspired by her own Nile cruise.
2000s
Luxury dahabiya (traditional sailing boats) cruises become a premium alternative.
Present
Over 500 cruise ships operate the Luxor-Aswan route, from budget to ultra-luxury.

Essential Info

Visitor Tips

Everything you need to know for a perfect visit.

📅

Book Early

Popular cruises sell out months in advance, especially during peak season (Oct-Apr).

🚢

Ship Selection

Choose upper-deck cabins for better views and less engine noise.

⬆️

Go Upstream

Aswan-to-Luxor routes sail against the current, providing a more relaxed pace.

🧳

Packing

Pack smart-casual for dinner and comfortable walking shoes for temple visits.

💰

Tipping

Budget $5-10/day per person for crew tips. It's customary and appreciated.

⛵

Dahabiya Option

For a premium experience, try a traditional dahabiya — intimate and wind-powered.
